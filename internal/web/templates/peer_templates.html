{{template "icon-info"}}What a template owns
A template is a peer without an identity: the role, policy chains, import limit, transport safeguards and export transforms a kind of session should have, kept once. Link peers to it and they take that shape; save the template and every linked peer is rewritten — thirty IX peers change in one edit. Each peer keeps its own name, neighbor, AS, password and enabled/drain switches. Nothing reaches the router until you review the result under Changes and apply.
{{template "icon-peers"}}Templates {{template "icon-plus"}}Add template

To capture the shape of a peer you already have, open that peer and choose Save as template. To add a session of a template's kind, use Add peer on its row — only the identity is left to type.
